# Prompt 6: Annual refresh

Use once a year, ideally a month before the annual review. Attach the current agenda, the updated CV, and the tracker's dashboard export or `data/` folder.

---

It is {{date}}, one year further into {{name}}'s {{horizon_years}}-year {{agenda_term}}. Refresh it.

1. **Scoreboard.** For every metric and outcome, last year's value, this year's value, and the target. Mark each on track, at risk, or off track, with one sentence of evidence.
2. **Gates.** Which gates passed, slipped, or were dropped, and why.
3. **Landscape.** Search for what changed in the year: programs started, ended, or renamed; new competitors, products, or results that touch the gap claims; deadlines and events for the next twelve months, each with a confidence level and source.
4. **Red-team deltas.** Re-run the reviewer's checks from prompt 3 on the parts that changed: new claims against the CV, gap claims against the new literature, capacity against this year's load.
5. **Decisions.** Up to five decisions for the next year, in the order to ask them, each with options and a recommendation. They will be asked one at a time.
6. **Revised sections.** Rewrite only the sections that change, and give a change log: `Section | What changed | Why`.
7. **Recognition check.** Against the discipline profile's ladder: the next milestone, whether the evidence for it is accumulating, and what would most strengthen the case this year.
