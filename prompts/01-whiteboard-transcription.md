# Prompt 1: Transcribe the whiteboard

Use right after the whiteboard session. Attach the photo. The faculty member checks the transcription before anything else runs, because every later prompt treats it as the faculty member's own statement of the problem.

---

The attached photo shows a whiteboard from a research-agenda working session with {{name}} ({{field}}). Transcribe it. Do not interpret, expand, or improve it; a later step does that.

Return exactly these parts:

1. **Layout.** One sentence on how the board is organized (columns, boxes, numbered items, arrows).
2. **Transcription.** Every piece of text, grouped by region, in this structure:
   - Field:
   - Area:
   - Topic:
   - Objective:
   - Numbered challenges (keep the board's numbering): the text, then any names, works, or notes written under it.
   - Arrows and relationships: what points to what.
   - Anything else on the board.
3. **Uncertain readings.** A table: `Where | What the board seems to say | Other possible readings`. Include every word you are less than sure of.
4. **Probable corrections.** A table: `Board text | Probable intended text | Why`. Use this for misspelled names and titles (a researcher's name, a paper's title). Do not apply the corrections in part 2.
5. **Questions for {{surname}}.** Up to five short questions that would resolve the uncertain readings or the gaps a drafter would otherwise fill by guessing (for example, a challenge with no works listed, or a phrase such as "no one looking").

Photo checklist for the facilitator, if the reading is poor: shoot straight on, fill the frame with the board, turn off glare-producing lights, and take a second close-up of any dense region.
