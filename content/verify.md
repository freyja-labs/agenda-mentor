An AI-drafted agenda fails in predictable places. A reviewer reads the CV right after the agenda, so the first unsupported claim costs the document its credibility. Run this checklist on version 2, and again after any major edit.

[[checklist]]

## Confidence levels for dates

Every external date in an agenda or tracker carries one of five levels, so a reader knows how much weight it bears.

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `verified` | Checked against the organizer's or funder's own page, on a stated date |
| `reported` | From a third-party listing |
| `derived` | Computed from a published rule (for example, "the last Monday in January") |
| `expected` | Projected from last year's cycle; replace it when the call is released |
| `record` | From the CV or award documents |

## Failure modes this checklist catches

| Failure | How it shows up | The check that catches it |
|---|---|---|
| Stale record | "Current" projects that ended, centers the CV does not list, programs written in the present tense | Claims ledger traced to the current CV |
| Overstated gap | "No tool does this" when two vendors sell one, or a systematization paper covers the area | Gap search before the gap is written |
| Aspiration as fact | "Steady output at the flagship venues" when the record shows one paper in ten years | Baseline first, then target |
| Engineless result | A make-or-break result with no funding, student, or code behind it | Engine check at months 6 and 12 |
| Undefined metric | A target with no test, statistic, unit, or baseline | Metric definitions |
| Overload | Five thrusts and seven community artifacts for one person with a full service load | Capacity section and gates |
| Missing competitor | Leader tables that omit the closest group | Nearest-group search and decision |
| Two taxonomies | The same labels defined twice, once from the CV and once from the board | One taxonomy, GC and S |
| Stale calendar | A deadline that passed, a cycle that closed, a venue that does not fit | Confidence levels and sources |
