Fields differ in what counts as the record, which honors mark a career, and how a community sets its agenda. A profile carries those differences into the prompts, so an education researcher and a hardware engineer get the same structure with their own field's conventions. Pick the closest profile; the general profile asks the model to identify your field's conventions and flags each one for your mentor to confirm.
