A research agenda states the hard problems a faculty member will lead their field on, a five-year plan to make progress, and the path from their current career stage toward the field's highest recognition. This method builds one in seven steps. The faculty member owns every decision. A mentor holds the process. AI drafts and checks, under rules that keep it honest about the record.

## What AI does here, and what stays with people

AI is good at three things in this process: turning a confirmed whiteboard and a CV into a complete first draft fast, searching the landscape on the day the draft is written, and playing a demanding reviewer. It is unreliable in three predictable ways. It fills silence with plausible content, so every item the whiteboard did not supply is labeled *Suggested:*. It reads public bios that lag reality, so every claim about the faculty member goes into a claims ledger tied to the current CV. And it writes aspirations in the present tense, so outcomes are always stated as a baseline from the record followed by a target.

People keep the parts that carry consequences: what to be known for, which competitor to collaborate with, which sponsor to approach, who gets named, and what to decline to make room for the plan.

## The community cycle

The agenda's long game is to become the place the field turns to when it decides what to fund next. The cycle is the same in every discipline; the profiles name each field's venues for it.

1. A sponsored working session brings the field's leaders together around the agenda's hard problems.
2. The participants co-author an agenda paper that program officers and reviewers can cite.
3. A guest-edited special issue collects the first answers.
4. A recurring workshop or symposium tracks progress each year, often around a shared benchmark, dataset, or challenge.
5. An edited volume or handbook consolidates the field's methods.
6. A standing group (a technical committee or special interest group) keeps the cycle running after any one grant ends.

Every step is gated. The working session waits for a committed sponsor and one accepted paper in a flagship venue; the later steps wait for the earlier ones. A junior faculty member who starts the cycle before the record supports it spends years of service time on a structure nobody else is ready to join.

## By career stage

**Assistant professors.** The make-or-break result anchors the tenure case and the first program-scale award. The recognition path runs through an early-career award and the field's flagship venues. Keep the community cycle to its first two steps, and lead them jointly with a senior colleague.

**Associate professors.** The make-or-break result anchors promotion to full professor and a center-scale proposal. The recognition path runs through a society fellowship. The full cycle is in reach once the gates open.

**Full professors.** The agenda is the case for the academy. The cycle is the core of the plan, and the recognition test six years out should describe a field that uses the faculty member's methods as its reference.
